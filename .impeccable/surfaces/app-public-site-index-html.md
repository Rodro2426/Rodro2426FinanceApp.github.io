# Pockets website (/site/)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: friends and family the owner sends the link to, mostly on iPhone via WhatsApp, some on a computer. Job: understand Pockets in one viewport, believe it's worth trying, open it or install it from Safari. Proof: the real app's screens (sample data, labeled). Constraints: Spanish first with an English switch; free, no store, no invented claims; works on phone and desktop; static files beside the app on GitHub Pages.

Chosen by the owner after three re-rolled structure rounds: "Just remake the Pockets Launch Film", with the steer "less bubbly, keep the vibe of the app, and a version for PC".

## Direction contract

THESIS: The launch film as a real website: one 3D iPhone running the real app turns around between features while you scroll. It refuses the bento grid of feature cards and the split hero with a badge row.

OWN-WORLD: The app's own surfaces: warm off-white ground #f7f5f3, white cards, near-black ink #201e1d, the app's red #ec3013 only on the primary action, pastel pocket tints (green, blue, rose, lilac, butter) washing the stage behind each scene, the indigo pocket logo, deep navy #232e7a for the closing frame. Rounded type (self-hosted Nunito, SF Pro Rounded on Apple). Floating objects are real app fragments (a pocket tile, a bill alert, the safe-to-spend chip), not clay balls.

STORY: See today's safe number on a real phone → watch eight features turn by on the same phone → see it on a computer too → the little things → three install steps → questions friends ask → open it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: left 5/12 headline at ~88px, one sentence, red Open Pockets button and a quiet "How to install" link; right 7/12 the tilted 3D phone showing Home with three app fragments floating at different depths. Phone: headline, sentence, button, then the phone below, cut by the fold. Amendment (after finish review): one quiet facts line under the actions, "Gratis · Sin App Store · Español e inglés", kept because it answers the three questions friends ask first.

FORM: Sticky story stage (launch film), user-pinned over the roll. seed: a83e201ef360c466

Signature interaction: the phone spins 360° on its Y axis when the active feature changes and the screen swaps while its back faces you; the stage tint crossfades to the scene's pocket colour. Interruptible (transition retargets), reduced motion becomes a 200ms crossfade.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
